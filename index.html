Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Helpful details before choosing a flood barrier for your home.

How much does Flood Barrier cost?

Our current target price is around ₱3,500. The final price depends on confirmed materials, dimensions, and production costs.

Will it completely stop flooding?

No product should be assumed to stop all flooding. This barrier is designed to help reduce water entering through a compatible doorway. Results depend on installation, water depth and pressure, the surrounding structure, and testing.

Will it fit every doorway?

Not necessarily. Measure your doorway first and confirm the supported width range and surface requirements before ordering.

Can I remove and store it?

The concept is intended to be removable and stored when not in use. Follow the final product's instructions for removal, cleaning, and storage.

When should I install it?

Prepare early and follow local weather alerts. Do not approach moving floodwater or attempt installation in dangerous conditions.
